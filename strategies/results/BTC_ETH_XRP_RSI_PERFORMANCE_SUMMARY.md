## 🎓 Educational Insights

### Why RSI Works on These Assets:

**BTC**:
- **Mean Reversion Strength**: BTC regularly overshoots/undershoots fair value
- **Liquidity**: Deep order books = RSI signals are reliable (not easily manipulated)
- **Market Maturity**: 15+ years of history = well-established price patterns
- **Institutional Interest**: Creates stronger mean reversion (buyers step in on dips)

**ETH**:
- **Technology Driven**: Network upgrades cause temporary price dislocations
- **DeFi Ecosystem**: ETH's utility creates fundamental support levels
- **Gas Fee Dynamics**: Sometimes creates irrational selling (mean reversion opportunities)
- **BTC Correlation with Independence**: Moves with BTC but has own dynamics

**XRP**:
- **News Sensitivity**: Ripple/SEC news causes overreactions (great for mean reversion)
- **Retail Dominated**: More emotional trading = stronger RSI extremes
- **Payment Use Case**: Creates fundamental floor (reversion support)
- **Volatility**: Higher volatility = more frequent RSI signals

### Why Dynamic Position Sizing Works:

**Strong Signals (RSI 10-20)**:
- More confident entry (very oversold)
- Larger position captures larger bounce
- Risk/reward heavily favors entry

**Weak Signals (RSI 28-30)**:
- Less confident (barely oversold)
- Smaller position limits loss if wrong
- Still captures move if it works

**Result**: Portfolio deploys capital proportionally to signal quality, maximizing risk-adjusted returns.
